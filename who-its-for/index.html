Questions about fit

Almost certainly. Published clients include ceramicists, sculptors, tattoo artists, photographers, MMA gyms, pilates studios, chiropractors, dentists, dermatologists, realtors, authors, coffee shops, supercar accounts, pet brands and sports-card sellers. Narrow niches do particularly well.

Does it work for local businesses?

Yes. Location targeting puts your account in front of people in the areas you actually serve, which is why cafés, studios and clinics appear throughout the client roster.

Is it right for a brand-new account?

Yes — some of the largest proportional gains start small. One artist went from 929 followers to 5,344, a 475% increase.

Can I use it for a business and a personal account?

Yes. Clients run Virallized across multiple accounts, and reviewers describe managing several as straightforward.
