How Virallized Reviews works

Who runs this site, where every figure comes from, and what it will not do.

Updated 25 October 2026 · Trustpilot figures read 25 October 2026 · Written by the Virallized team

Short version: Virallized Reviews is run by Virallized, one of the Instagram growth services it reviews. Every rating on the site comes from a public Trustpilot profile and links to it, and every rule or law it mentions links to the page that publishes it.

It is a first-party publication, so it is written to be checked rather than trusted: the date of every read is on the page, and the sources are one click away.

Who runs this site

Virallized Reviews is owned and run by Virallized, the Instagram growth service at virallized.com. Virallized is one of the services this site reviews and ranks, and it competes with every other service listed. A line at the top of every page says so.

That makes this a first-party publication, not an independent review site. Read the rankings knowing who wrote them, and use the source links to check any figure for yourself.

The US Federal Trade Commission’s rule on fake reviews and testimonials bars a business from presenting a review site it controls as independent. This site does not, and says who runs it on every page.

Where every figure comes from

Ratings and review counts are read from each company’s public Trustpilot profile, most recently on 25 October 2026, and every one links to that profile. Trustpilot is not affiliated with Virallized, and Virallized cannot remove or edit reviews there.

Prices, guarantees and method are taken from each company’s own website and terms, in its own words where the page quotes them.

Critical-review themes come from reading each service’s one- and two-star reviews. Quotes are verbatim, attributed to the reviewer and dated, and link back to the source.

Platform rules and the law are quoted or summarised from the pages that publish them: Instagram’s Help Centre, Meta’s Transparency Centre and the US Federal Trade Commission, each read on 25 October 2026 and listed under “Sources checked” on the pages that cite them.

Virallized’s own growth figures come from its campaign records: In a 200-account sample of Virallized client campaigns that had run at least 90 days, measured to 23 October 2026, the median account added 407 net followers a month and all 200 finished larger than they started. That is Virallized measuring itself, so the site says so wherever it appears.

What this site does not do

It does not sell placements. No company pays to be listed, ranked or left out, and the site carries no affiliate links.

It does not publish test results it has not run. This site has not bought competitors’ plans, so it reports their public records rather than inventing a trial with numbers attached. Where a page describes what a customer experienced, it quotes that customer’s own review.

It does not hide who wrote it, and it does not present itself as independent.

How the rankings work

Services are scored on a published model that weights independently checkable evidence, such as public ratings and named client accounts, above marketing claims. The criteria, weights and limits are on the methodology page. Virallized scores highest on that model, and the ranking shows every input so the result can be checked.

Inclusion is limited to services that sell Instagram audience growth, confirmed by reading each company’s own description of its service. Follower sellers and general social media tools are a different category and are left out.

Updates and corrections

Every page shows the date it last changed and the date its Trustpilot figures were read. Ratings are re-read before each update.

If a figure or description is wrong, write to support@virallized.com with the source. Companies listed here are welcome to do the same, and substantiated corrections are made promptly. The full terms of use cover the rest.
