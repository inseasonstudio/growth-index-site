In a 200-account sample of Virallized client campaigns that had run at least 90 days, measured to 23 October 2026, the median account added 407 net followers a month and all 200 finished larger than they started. Net means after every unfollow and every account Instagram removed. Nothing was excluded, and four in five were on the Standard or Pro plan.

  • What a median means. Half the accounts added more than 407 a month and half added fewer. At the median pace, 1,000 new followers takes about two and a half months; that is arithmetic on the median, not a measured time.
  • Size changes the percentage. 407 a month is 20% of a 2,000-follower account and 2% of a 20,000-follower one.
  • Who measured it. Virallized, from its own campaign records, so it is not an independent audit. The Trustpilot record is the independent check, and your own account is the final one.
  • Check it on your own account. Instagram’s free insights show an account’s performance for any period in the last 90 days, so a first month can be measured on the account itself instead of on anyone’s sales page.

Pricing questions

How much does Virallized cost?

Plans run from $99 a month for Standard up to $299 a month for Managed, with Pro at $149 and Max at $199. Annual billing saves around 25%.

Which plan should I choose?

Pro at $149 a month is the most popular and the best balance for most accounts, adding a personal account manager and the latest AI targeting on top of Standard. Choose Max or Managed for the fastest growth, Standard if you are starting out.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes — a 7-day money-back guarantee on every plan, no exceptions and no small print.

Is there a contract?

No. No contracts, no lock-in, cancel at any time.

Is there a free trial?

There is a free growth analysis that needs no login and no card — drop in your handle and you get your real numbers, engagement rate, how you rank against top accounts your size, and a growth projection. Every paid plan is then backed by the 7-day guarantee.

What is the Strategy Audit?

An optional $79 add-on: a deeper review of your account and content strategy alongside your growth campaign, available on any plan.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled securely through Stripe.
