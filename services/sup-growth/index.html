How to check Sup Growth yourself

  • Before paying. Open Sup Growth’s Trustpilot profile, sort by most recent and read the one- and two-star reviews first; they say more about cancelling and refunds than the headline score does.
  • On day one. Sup Growth’s published terms: 14-day free trial; a “money-back guarantee” is promised without published terms. Note your follower count and the reach of your last few posts, then compare them later in Instagram’s free insights, which cover any period in the last 90 days.
  • After the first week. Open Account Status in Instagram’s settings. Instagram says it shows whether anything on the account goes against its rules and may lead to restrictions.
