Questions buyers ask about Kenji

Is Kenji legit?

Kenji is a real, trading service with a public Trustpilot profile carrying 2.0/5 from 28 reviews. On distribution, 11% of its reviews are five-star and 68% are one-star. Its critical reviewers most often report followers reported as fake, billing and cancellation traps, refunds declined. Read the profile directly before deciding — it is linked on this page.

Does Kenji use real followers or bots?

Kenji states it delivers real, organic followers. Critical reviewers dispute this: Reviewers describe accounts with generic usernames, no posts and no followers. Their quotes are reproduced above, unedited.

How much does Kenji cost?

Pricing runs $19 to $64/mo. Buyer protection: 3-day free trial.

Can you cancel Kenji?

Reviewers report being charged for a fresh period after reactivating, and login failures around renewal dates. Anyone subscribing should check the cancellation route before paying.

Is Kenji safe for my Instagram account?

No account suspension pattern appears in Kenji's published review record. It describes its method as automated / software; automated activity is generally the higher-risk approach on Instagram.
