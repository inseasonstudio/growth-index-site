How to check UpGrow yourself

  • Before paying. UpGrow’s Trustpilot profile carries a warning instead of a rating. Trustpilot places these when a business misuses the platform, for example by fabricating reviews.
  • On day one. UpGrow’s published terms: Free trial; a full monthly refund if there is no net-positive growth after the first 7 days, per its Terms. Note your follower count and the reach of your last few posts, then compare them later in Instagram’s free insights, which cover any period in the last 90 days.
  • After the first week. Open Account Status in Instagram’s settings. Instagram says it shows whether anything on the account goes against its rules and may lead to restrictions.

Questions buyers ask about UpGrow

Is UpGrow legit?

UpGrow is a real, trading service, but it has no Trustpilot rating: Trustpilot suspended it for a breach of its guidelines, with 3,298 reviews still visible. On distribution, 43% of its reviews are five-star and 45% are one-star. Its critical reviewers most often report charged after cancelling, no way to cancel online, follower orders not delivered. Read the profile directly before deciding — it is linked on this page.

Does UpGrow use real followers or bots?

UpGrow states it delivers real followers. Its published review record does not show a recurring pattern of bot-follower complaints.

How much does UpGrow cost?

Pricing runs $69 to $129/mo. Buyer protection: Free trial; a full monthly refund if there is no net-positive growth after the first 7 days, per its Terms.

Can you cancel UpGrow?

Several reviewers report charges continuing for months after a cancellation they say was confirmed. Reviewers say there is no cancellation page despite the site’s promise of cancelling with one button, so cancelling means contacting support. Anyone subscribing should check the cancellation route before paying.

Is UpGrow safe for my Instagram account?

No account suspension pattern appears in UpGrow's published review record. It describes its method as not disclosed; automated activity is generally the higher-risk approach on Instagram.
