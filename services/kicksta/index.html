Questions buyers ask about Kicksta

Is Kicksta legit?

Kicksta is a real, trading service with a public Trustpilot profile carrying 2.0/5 from 626 reviews. On distribution, 35% of its reviews are five-star and 41% are one-star. Its critical reviewers most often report followers reported as bots, difficulty cancelling, refunds declined. Read the profile directly before deciding — it is linked on this page.

Does Kicksta use real followers or bots?

Kicksta states it delivers real, organic followers. Critical reviewers dispute this: Reviewers describe followers arriving in timed batches from accounts created within a day, many with exactly six posts. Their quotes are reproduced above, unedited.

How much does Kicksta cost?

Pricing runs $69 to $129/mo. Buyer protection: 7-day free trial; “guaranteed growth or a trial extension” — not a refund.

Can you cancel Kicksta?

Multiple reviewers report cancellation requests going unanswered, or being unable to cancel without contacting support. Anyone subscribing should check the cancellation route before paying.

Is Kicksta safe for my Instagram account?

One reviewer reports a permanent Instagram deactivation they attribute to the tool. Kicksta describes its method as automated / software.
