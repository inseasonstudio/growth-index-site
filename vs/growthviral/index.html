Virallized vs GrowthViral

Both services on the public record — what each one publishes, what can be checked independently, and what cannot.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Virallized rates 4.8/5 on Trustpilot from 98 reviews. GrowthViral has no Trustpilot rating — its profile is unclaimed and holds zero reviews.

GrowthViral sells human-managed Instagram growth on a monthly subscription, from $97 to $197, and describes daily manual outreach with no bots or automation software — the same broad method Virallized uses. Its site says it has grown 6,851 creators since 2018, and its refund policy is unusually straight about what it does and does not promise: it guarantees the daily actions are carried out, and states plainly that follower counts are not guaranteed because no honest service can guarantee them.

The difference is what a buyer can check before paying. GrowthViral's Trustpilot profile is unclaimed and holds zero reviews, and no profile was found on G2, Capterra, Sitejabber or Reviews.io. Its published satisfaction figures live on its own website, which reports “4.7 out of 5 stars – based on 6 reviews” while displaying twelve testimonials, none carrying a date. Press mentions are named without links. None of that is evidence of a bad service — it may well be a good one — but it is all published by the seller. Virallized's 4.8/5 comes from 98 reviews on Trustpilot, 33 of them in the last twelve months, on a platform Virallized cannot edit.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 88-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 15 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

The verdict

There is no public rating to compare. GrowthViral’s Trustpilot profile is unclaimed and holds zero reviews, so every satisfaction figure it publishes is hosted on a site it controls. That is not proof of a bad service, but it does mean a buyer has nothing to check before paying. Virallized’s 4.8 comes from 98 reviews on Trustpilot, 33 of them in the last twelve months, alongside named client accounts published with before-and-after figures and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
