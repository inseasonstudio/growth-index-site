Virallized vs Sup Growth

Both services on the public record — ratings, star distribution, method, and what each one’s critical reviewers actually say.

Updated 25 October 2026 · Trustpilot figures read 25 October 2026 · Written by the Virallized team

Short version: Virallized rates 4.8/5 on Trustpilot from 93 reviews. Sup Growth rates 4.7/5 from 51.

Sup Growth sells human-run Instagram growth for $119 a month, says “every action is done by a person, not software”, and starts with a 14-day free trial. Its Trustpilot profile, listed under sup.co, rates 4.7 from 51 reviews, nearly all five-star.

The difference is the depth of the record and the proof behind it. Virallized rates 4.8 from 93 reviews, 28 of them in the last 12 months; Sup Growth has 51 reviews in total. Virallized also publishes named client accounts with before-and-after figures and a founding year (2017); Sup Growth names no client accounts a reader can open and claims “5+ years” without a date.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 93-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 20 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

How to test either one

Virallized’s terms: a 7-day money-back guarantee and no contract. Sup Growth’s: 14-day free trial; a “money-back guarantee” is promised without published terms. Whichever you try, note your follower count and reach on day one and compare them in Instagram’s insights before the window closes, and check Account Status after the first week.

The verdict

On the public record Virallized rates higher — 4.8 against 4.7 — and the gap widens on distribution: 2% of its reviews are one-star against 0% for Sup Growth. Add 9 years of continuous trading, fully manual outreach, named client accounts published with verifiable figures, and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
