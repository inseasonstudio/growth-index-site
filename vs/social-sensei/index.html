Both services on the public record — ratings, star distribution, method, and what each one’s critical reviewers actually say.

What Social Sensei’s critical reviewers report

10% of Social Sensei’s lifetime reviews are rated one star. Rather than characterise that second-hand, here are the recurring themes and the reviewers’ own words, each attributed and linked to source.

  • Followers reported as bots Several reviewers describe the followers as bots, spam or giveaway accounts, or from countries they did not target.
  • Charged before delivery One reviewer reports three weeks of a pending order and a second month's charge before the service started.
  • Refunds refused Reviewers report refund requests being turned down.
  • Disorganised service Missed reports, changing account staff and slow communication recur.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 93-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 20 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

How to test either one

Virallized’s terms: a 7-day money-back guarantee and no contract. Social Sensei’s: “14 Days Money Back Guarantee”; after that, its FAQ says partial refunds “may be offered” for unused days. Whichever you try, note your follower count and reach on day one and compare them in Instagram’s insights before the window closes, and check Account Status after the first week. If Social Sensei’s critical reviewers are right about bot followers, Instagram’s Potential spam list lets you remove them in one go.

The verdict

On the public record Virallized rates higher — 4.8 against 3.1 — and the gap widens on distribution: 2% of its reviews are one-star against 10% for Social Sensei. Add 9 years of continuous trading, fully manual outreach, named client accounts published with verifiable figures, and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
