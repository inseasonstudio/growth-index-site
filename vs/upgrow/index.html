Virallized vs UpGrow

Both services on the public record — what each one publishes, what can be checked independently, and what cannot.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Virallized rates 4.8/5 on Trustpilot from 98 reviews. UpGrow has no Trustpilot rating: Trustpilot suspended it for a breach of its guidelines. Its profile reads “This company’s rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines.” and “We’ve removed a number of fake reviews for this company.”

UpGrow carries the largest review base in this directory, 3,770 reviews on Trustpilot, but no rating: Trustpilot has suspended it after removing fake reviews. Of the reviews still visible, 42% are one-star, and 3,167 of them were written in the last twelve months. Alongside its growth plans, its Quick Shop sells Instagram followers, likes and views outright.

Virallized rates 4.8 from 98 reviews, accumulated steadily since 2023, with named public client accounts that can be inspected before buying. It sells organic growth only: no follower, like or view packages.

What UpGrow’s critical reviewers report

42% of UpGrow’s visible reviews are rated one star. Rather than characterise that second-hand, here are the recurring themes and the reviewers’ own words, each attributed and linked to source.

  • Charged after cancelling Several reviewers report charges continuing for months after a cancellation they say was confirmed.
  • No way to cancel online Reviewers say there is no cancellation page despite the site’s promise of cancelling with one button, so cancelling means contacting support.
  • Follower orders not delivered Buyers from its Quick Shop report paid orders that were not fulfilled.
  • No public replies None of the 40 most recent one- and two-star reviews carries a reply from the company.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 98-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 18 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

The verdict

There is no rating to compare: Trustpilot has suspended UpGrow’s after removing fake reviews. What remains visible is 3,770 reviews, 42% of them one-star and 3,167 written in the last twelve months. Virallized’s 4.8 comes from 98 reviews, 97% of them five-star, alongside named client accounts published with before-and-after figures and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
