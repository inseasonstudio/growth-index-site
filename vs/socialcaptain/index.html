What SocialCaptain’s critical reviewers report

94% of SocialCaptain’s lifetime reviews are rated one star. Rather than characterise that second-hand, here are the recurring themes and the reviewers’ own words, each attributed and linked to source.

  • Charges after cancellation Reviewers report continued billing after cancelling, and refunds confirmed but never received.
  • Followers unfollow over time Reviewers report delivered followers dropping away day by day.
  • Service stopped functioning Reviewers report the product ceasing to work while still being billed.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 93-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 17 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

The verdict

On the public record Virallized rates higher — 4.8 against 1.2 — and the gap widens on distribution: 2% of its reviews are one-star against 94% for SocialCaptain. Add 9 years of continuous trading, fully manual outreach, named client accounts published with verifiable figures, and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
