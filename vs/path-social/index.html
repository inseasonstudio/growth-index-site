Virallized vs Path Social

Both services on the public record — what each one publishes, what can be checked independently, and what cannot.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Virallized rates 4.8/5 on Trustpilot from 98 reviews. Path Social has no Trustpilot rating: Trustpilot suspended it for a breach of its guidelines. Its profile reads “This company’s rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines.” and “We’ve removed a number of fake reviews for this company.”

Path Social carries one of the largest review bases in Instagram growth, 1,399 reviews on Trustpilot, but no rating: Trustpilot has suspended it after removing fake reviews. Of the reviews still visible, 78% are one-star.

Virallized rates 4.8 from 98 reviews. The gap is instructive: review volume measures how many people have used a service, not how many were happy. Both sell organic Instagram growth; the difference shows up in the distribution, where 97% of Virallized's reviews are five-star.

What Path Social’s critical reviewers report

78% of Path Social’s visible reviews are rated one star. Rather than characterise that second-hand, here are the recurring themes and the reviewers’ own words, each attributed and linked to source.

  • Followers reported as bots Reviewers describe fake accounts arriving in volume, in some cases prompting Instagram warnings.
  • Contracted volumes not met A reviewer on an annual plan reports growth far below the minimum stated at purchase.
  • Refunds declined Several reviewers report refunds refused after raising delivery complaints.
  • Account safety One reviewer reports their username being blocked within hours of subscribing.

What the Virallized record shows on the same measures

  • Follower authenticity No reviewer in the 98-review record reports bot or fake followers. The most repeated theme is the opposite — that gained followers were real, active accounts that engaged and stayed.
  • Account safety No account suspension, ban or shadowban attributable to the service appears anywhere in the record, across nine years of clients.
  • Cancelling Reviewers who paused or cancelled describe it as straightforward, with one noting there was “no pressure to stay” and that the team helped them wind down.
  • Refunds A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to every plan, with no contract and no lock-in.
  • Account access Virallized never posts to a client’s account and never sends DMs on their behalf.
  • One-star share 2% of the lifetime record — the lowest of the 18 services in this directory.

Each of these is checkable on the same public platform the figures above come from.

The verdict

There is no rating to compare: Trustpilot has suspended Path Social’s after removing fake reviews. What remains visible is 1,399 reviews, 78% of them one-star and 216 written in the last twelve months. Virallized’s 4.8 comes from 98 reviews, 97% of them five-star, alongside named client accounts published with before-and-after figures and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
