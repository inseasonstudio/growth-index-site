Instagram growth fundamentals

What is a good engagement rate on Instagram?

Typically 1–3% is average, and above 3% is strong. Smaller accounts usually run higher rates than large ones, because a tighter audience engages more consistently. The figure that matters is your own trend over time rather than a benchmark — if it is falling while followers rise, the followers are not engaging.

Does buying followers lower your engagement rate?

Yes, and it is simple arithmetic. Engagement rate is engagement divided by followers. Adding followers who never engage enlarges the denominator and nothing else, so the rate falls. Since Instagram uses engagement to decide how widely to distribute a post, reach falls with it.

Is it better to have 1,000 engaged followers or 10,000 inactive ones?

1,000 engaged, without much argument. A thousand followers at 10% engagement produces more reach, more comments and more commercial outcomes than ten thousand at 0.5% — and brands assessing a partnership look at engagement rate before follower count.

How often should I post on Instagram to grow?

Consistency matters more than volume, and format matters more than either. Most growth comes from Reels, because they are the main format shown to people who do not already follow you. Several posts a week sustained beats a burst followed by silence.

Do Reels grow your account faster than regular posts?

Generally yes, because they carry most non-follower distribution. Static posts largely reach people who already follow you, which builds engagement but not audience. If follower growth is the goal, Reels are the lever.

Do hashtags still work for Instagram growth?

They help categorise content but they are no longer a primary discovery route. Instagram's recommendation system reads the content itself — audio, captions, subject matter — far more heavily than tags. A handful of relevant hashtags is sensible; thirty generic ones is not a growth strategy.

How many followers do you need to make money on Instagram?

Less than most people assume, if the audience is relevant. Brands increasingly work with accounts in the 5,000–20,000 range because engagement rates are higher and audiences more specific. A tightly-defined audience of a few thousand can out-earn a broad one of fifty.

How many followers do you need for brand deals?

There is no threshold, but engagement rate and niche clarity carry more weight than count. Reviewers in this record report partnerships and leads at follower counts from around 5,000 upward, where the audience matched the brand.

Why did my Instagram reach suddenly drop?

Most often a format shift or an engagement-ratio change rather than a penalty. Check the share of accounts reached who do not already follow you: if that has fallen, discovery is the problem. Posting fewer Reels is the single most common cause.

How long should I give a growth strategy before judging it?

At least a month for signal, three for a verdict. Documented campaigns in this category show the largest gains compounding over three to six months, and week-to-week variation is noise rather than trend.

Does follower count or engagement matter more to the algorithm?

Engagement, by a wide margin. Instagram distributes content according to how people respond to it, not how many follow the account. This is why a large but inactive following actively suppresses reach rather than helping it.
