What is the best Kicksta alternative?

By independent Trustpilot rating, the strongest-rated alternatives are Virallized (4.8/5 from 93 reviews), Sup Growth (4.7/5 from 51 reviews), Insfamous (4.2/5 from 98 reviews), Social Boost (4.0/5 from 393 reviews). Kicksta itself rates 1.9/5 from 629 reviews.

Why do people look for Kicksta alternatives?

Kicksta pairs one of the biggest names in Instagram growth with one of the lowest ratings in the field. It rates 1.9 out of 5 across 629 Trustpilot reviews — a large enough base for the average to be meaningful, which is usually what sends people looking elsewhere.

What should I check before choosing any growth service?

Four things, all public: the Trustpilot profile read directly rather than quoted; how long the company has traded; whether it names real client accounts you can open on Instagram; and whether the method is manual or automated, since automation is what tends to trigger account problems.
