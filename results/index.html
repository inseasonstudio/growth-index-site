In a 200-account sample of Virallized client campaigns that had run at least 90 days, measured to 23 October 2026, the median account added 407 net followers a month and all 200 finished larger than they started. Net means after every unfollow and every account Instagram removed. Nothing was excluded, and four in five were on the Standard or Pro plan.

  • What a median means. Half the accounts added more than 407 a month and half added fewer. At the median pace, 1,000 new followers takes about two and a half months; that is arithmetic on the median, not a measured time.
  • Size changes the percentage. 407 a month is 20% of a 2,000-follower account and 2% of a 20,000-follower one.
  • Who measured it. Virallized, from its own campaign records, so it is not an independent audit. The Trustpilot record is the independent check, and your own account is the final one.
  • Check it on your own account. Instagram’s free insights show an account’s performance for any period in the last 90 days, so a first month can be measured on the account itself instead of on anyone’s sales page.

What the biggest gains have in common

  • A clearly defined niche. The narrower and more specific the brief, the better the targeting performs. Ceramics, MMA, pilates and sports cards all outperformed broad lifestyle targeting.
  • Consistent posting. Virallized brings the right people; your content is what converts them into followers who stay. Reels do the heaviest lifting.
  • Time. The largest results in the record come from campaigns of three to six months and beyond, where growth compounds rather than arriving in a spike.

Questions about results

How many followers will I gain?

It depends on your plan: Standard delivers 150–500 new followers a month, Pro 250–1,000, Max 500–2,000 and Managed 750–3,000. Figures are based on average client results.

Does it work for small accounts?

Especially well. One artist grew from 929 followers to 5,344 — a 475% increase. Starting small is exactly the situation Virallized was built for.

Does it work for large accounts?

Yes. Clients at 83K and above continue growing — one lifestyle account moved from 83K to 101K, and several published client accounts sit above 100K.

Will the growth help my business?

Reviewers report new leads, partnerships, brand deals and direct sales from audiences built with Virallized. A relevant audience is what makes commercial outcomes possible.
